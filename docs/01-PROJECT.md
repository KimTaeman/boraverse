# PROJECT — Scope, audience and non-negotiables

## Identity
- **Working name:** The Little Boraverse.
- **Event:** BTS ARIRANG Bangkok 2026, first show Dec 3; also Dec 5 and Dec 6.
- **Creator:** Independent ARMY fan (not affiliated with BTS/BIGHIT/HYBE or venue).
- **Goal:** Transform 2,000 inexpensive handmade freebie cards into meaningful, fun, gorgeous, shareable digital moments.
- **Tagline candidate:** Seven hearts. Countless memories. One little universe.
- **Core promise:** A little card leads to laughter, music, discovery, and a shared purple memory.

## Who this serves
1. **15-second visitor:** scanning while walking in crowded concert area. Needs immediate card reward and no signup.
2. **Curious fan:** wants games, random BTS song, fan jokes and collectible results.
3. **Sentimental ARMY:** wants Magic Shop letter, togetherness, Purple Ocean remembrance.
4. **Friend group:** compares gacha and fortunes and shares downloadable cards.
5. **Newer/casual fan:** may not recognize all references. Should still understand UI and optional explanations.

## Product principles
- **Cute + chaotic + meaningful + accessible**; no fake-virus-themed warnings or alarming system jargon.
- **Fast first value:** QR-specific reveal without long mandatory intro.
- **Magic + function:** illustrated spaces with familiar direct navigation, not hidden click-only artwork.
- **No account, no payment, no collection of names.** Optional local nickname typed into a downloadable certificate is NOT posted to server.
- **Share if wanted:** never gate freebies behind follows, reposts, permissions or giving data.
- **Phone-first, low-bandwidth, no autoplay audio** (concert area is noisy).
- **No copyrighted song/audio uploads**. Link to official listening platforms.
- **Asset replacement:** original illustration will be added later; placeholders must be straightforward to swap.

## Success criteria
- Printed QR opens correct HTTPS route reliably with physical card scanned by real devices.
- First meaningful collectible appears quickly on typical mobile network; no forced onboarding.
- Purple Ocean successfully stores and acknowledges contributions, with retries and honest counts.
- Main screens work at 320–430px and with reduced motion, screen reader, keyboard.
- Downloadable keepsake works; core site remains useful without database.
- User can explore site without confusion and return to main rooms easily.

## Constraints
- ~2,000 physical pieces across 8 groups, all have QR; no further purchases currently expected.
- Printing: university Brother MFC-T920DW, owner says existing 215gsm inkjet glossy has been tested successfully.
- Existing inventory: ~100 A4 215gsm glossy; 100 A4 150gsm; 600 transparent card sleeves 65×90mm; thin 80–100gsm supply quantity not fully counted; cutting/storage already owned.
- The exact art style, character illustrations, content variants, final production URL, and domain are **not finalized**.
- First public target is Dec 3, 2026. Prioritize core and QR reliability over expanding features.

## Non-goals for launch
No purchase flow, personal accounts, leaderboards, realtime chat, open unmoderated message boards, AI-generated conversations mimicking BTS, backend audio hosting, NFTs, digital currencies with real value, exact 1-person-1-light guarantees.

## Product shape
One mobile-first web app with: **Magical Village (home)**, **Purple Ocean (signature shared art)**, **Bangtan Arcade** (gacha + music), **Magic Shop** (fortunes + letters), **My Keepsakes** (on-device downloads), **Memory Garden** (later). All physical QR variants deep-link to a matching reveal but never lock features permanently.
